# Bounded-Round Gossip with Cluster-State Normalization, Replay-Weighted Proposals, and Pairwise Quorum Verification

**Type:** `Distributed Systems & Consensus Protocols (Section 5.4 Re-Enactment)` | **Structural Isomorphism Energy **$E_\theta$**:** `0.95`


$$
πθ(S|A,W) = exp(−Eθ(S,A,W))/Zθ(A,W),  Zθ = Σ_S exp(−Eθ(S,A,W));  q(S) = Σ_m ρ_m q_m(S),  Ẑ = (1/M)Σ_r exp(−Eθ(S_r,A,W))/q(S_r), S_r ∼ q;  P*(τ|S⁰) ∝ Q₀(τ|S⁰)exp(−βEθ(Sᴷ,A,W)),  Lᴷᴸ = KL(Qφ(τ|S⁰)||P*) = E_Qφ[βEθ(Sᴷ,A,W) + log(Qφ/Q₀)] + const;  Eθ = Σ_i s_i uθ(S_i,W) + λΣ_{i<j}h_ij softplus(−gθ(S_i,S_j,W))
$$


Let S denote a cluster-wide protocol state, A the active-node and policy context, and W the workload and network conditions. The protocol assigns each admissible state an energy Eθ(S,A,W), which can combine local validity costs with pairwise compatibility costs. Its normalized cluster-state distribution is πθ(S|A,W) = exp(−Eθ(S,A,W))/Zθ(A,W), where Zθ(A,W) sums the weights of all admissible cluster states. This global normalizer accounts for the competition among possible states; local node scores alone do not provide the normalized cluster-level probability.

Because enumerating the full state space is generally infeasible, estimate the normalizer using a proposal mixture q(S) = Σ_m ρ_m q_m(S). Components can include live gossip samples, replayed states from prior incidents, and states generated by controlled fault-injection runs. For samples S_r drawn from q, the importance-weighted estimate is Ẑ = (1/M)Σ_r exp(−Eθ(S_r,A,W))/q(S_r). The mixture should retain support for relevant admissible states, including rare but valid recovery configurations, so replay does not collapse exploration onto common operating conditions.

For a bounded-round rollout, let τ = (S¹,…,Sᴷ) be a K-round gossip trajectory beginning at S⁰. Define a target trajectory distribution P*(τ|S⁰) proportional to the baseline rollout distribution Q₀(τ|S⁰), tilted by the terminal-state energy exp(−βEθ(Sᴷ,A,W)). A rollout policy Qφ can be shaped toward this target by minimizing KL(Qφ||P*), equivalently the expected terminal energy plus the log-ratio penalty log(Qφ/Q₀), up to a constant. This formulation favors low-energy terminal states while penalizing departures from the baseline transition behavior.

Pairwise consistency is checked through quorum-certificate verification. For nodes i and j, gθ(S_i,S_j,W) scores whether their signed views, epochs, and commit records are mutually compatible. A gate h_ij = s_i s_j weights the pairwise check by the nodes’ participation or trust scores, while local terms uθ assess each node’s own state. The resulting cluster energy can aggregate local validity and gated pairwise certificate checks. A rollout is accepted only when its terminal state satisfies the configured quorum and safety constraints; the energy score guides selection among candidate states but does not replace deterministic certificate validation.
